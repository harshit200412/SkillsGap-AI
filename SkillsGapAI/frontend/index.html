Four steps, and no motivational speech

Most people guess at what they're missing and then study the wrong thing for six months. This is an attempt to stop that.

1

Show us your resume

Scored for keyword coverage, weak bullets, missing sections and the formatting that breaks parsers.

2

Pick the role

Eight tracks across software, business and core engineering, each built from live postings.

3

Read the gap

Every skill sorted into strong, moderate and critical against what employers actually ask for.

4

Work the plan

Weekly milestones with resources, practice and one piece of portfolio work per phase.

Three tracks. Not everyone writes code.

A civil engineer's gap is Primavera and quantity surveying. An MBA's is SQL and a number on every bullet. The report should know the difference.
